  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first; every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. Every app and email report, and form reports from booking-v2 tenants.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not touch this.
  • shift: the date moves one day earlier, weekday and all; the month never swaps. The desk spotted these (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant. It looks like timezone handling: one meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto.

How each report is placed

By where it came from, not by what intake guessed. Intake’s parser/hint tag comes from the reporter’s browser, and the tenant decides the cause (src/lib/kb/causes.ts). The desk’s reading of a report as a one-day move comes first, so no report counts twice (src/lib/kb/problems.ts). The export repeats some reports across pages; each id counts once.
